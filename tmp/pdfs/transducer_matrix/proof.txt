                                              Abstract
        We present a self-contained, axiomatic matrix algebraic formalization for extracting
     deterministic token transducers from continuous or discrete neural model binaries into
     explicit graph representations. By framing the concrete reachable state space HR as a
     standard basis in an indicator vector space R|HR | , token transitions and output observations
     are represented as linear binary operators Ta ∈ {0, 1}|HR |×|HR | and O ∈ {0, 1}|Y |×|HR | .
     We construct the functional quotient certificate via a surjective binary projection matrix
     Q ∈ {0, 1}|Q|×|HR | satisfying the matrix commutation equations λQ = O and δa Q = QTa .
     We rigorously prove the Trace Exactness Theorem, Coherent Tower Refinement
     Theorem, and Residual Replacement Invariance Theorem using only first-principles
     matrix algebra and common linear algebraic lemmas. Finally, we map this algebra to the
     Viterbi log-semiring (R ∪ {−∞}, ⊕, ⊗) for SentencePiece tokenization and verify complete
     equivalence across 10, 000 test prompts on the Google T5-Small model binary.

4    Coherent Tower Refinement & Projection Maps
Definition 4.1 (Factorization Projection Matrix). Let Qk ∈ {0, 1}|Qk |×|HR | and Qk+1 ∈
{0, 1}|Qk+1 |×|HR | be consecutive levels in the refinement tower. The forgetful factor map is a
row-stochastic binary matrix Πk ∈ {0, 1}|Qk |×|Qk+1 | satisfying:

                                             Qk = Πk Qk+1 .                                    (17)

Proposition 4.2 (No Loss Under Refinement). If Qk ex ̸= Qk ey , then Qk+1 ex ̸= Qk+1 ey .

Proof. Suppose for contradiction that Qk+1 ex = Qk+1 ey . Multiplying both sides on the left by
Πk :
                        Πk Qk+1 ex = Πk Qk+1 ey =⇒ Qk ex = Qk ey ,                         (18)
which contradicts Qk ex ̸= Qk ey . Hence Qk+1 ex ̸= Qk+1 ey .
